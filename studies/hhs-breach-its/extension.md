## Proposed extension (from the register)

US healthcare breaches 2010–2026 from the HHS OCR portal: interrupted time-series and changepoint analysis of hacking/ransomware, with lessons for India's DPDP breach-notification rules

## Why it is new

Prior study ended in 2024 and was descriptive. Adds 2025–2026 data, formal changepoint/ITS models, entity-type and business-associate breakdown, LLM-assisted ransomware tagging validated on a hand-labelled sample, and the first DPDP-compliance translation for Indian hospitals.

## Design

- **Study type:** Retrospective secondary-data analysis
- **Reporting guideline:** RECORD / STROBE (adapted)
- **Scores:** feasibility 5/5, novelty 3/5

The systematic gap analysis (novelty check per gap, open-data matches, design sketches) has not been done yet.
