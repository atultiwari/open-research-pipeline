## Proposed extension (from the register)

Adversarial vulnerability and cheap defences for patch-level pathology foundation models in MIL pipelines (Camelyon16/TCGA), with cross-FM transferability

## Why it is new

Original attacked slide-level FMs (CHIEF, PRISM, TITAN): ~0.1% perturbed patches dropped TITAN Camelyon16 accuracy from 98% to 42%. Extends to patch-level FMs (Phikon-v2, H-optimus, UNI2) under ABMIL/CLAM, transferability between FMs, JPEG/stain-normalisation defences, and a pathologist's threat-plausibility view.
