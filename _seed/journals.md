# Zero-APC Journal Shortlist (seed, researched 2026-11-04)

Source: exported from Atul's Claude Doc "Zero-APC Journal Shortlist". Figures come from publisher pages on 2026-11-04. "Verify" = the page did not load during research.

## Rules
- Publish free by choosing the subscription (non-open-access) route at acceptance in a hybrid journal. India gets no automatic APC waiver from Research4Life, Springer Nature, Wiley, Taylor & Francis or Wolters Kluwer, so fully open-access journals are excluded.
- NMC Medical Institutions (Qualifications of Faculty) Regulations 2025 count only original papers, meta-analyses, systematic reviews and case series in journals indexed in MEDLINE, PMC, SCI/SCIE, Embase, Scopus or DOAJ. Associate Professor → Professor needs at least two such papers after becoming Associate Professor, as one of the first three authors. Source: https://recruitment.ggsmch.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Notified-Medical-Institutions-Qualifications-of-Faculty-Regulations-2025.pdf
- UGC discontinued the UGC-CARE list in February 2025.

Free option outside these areas: Indian Journal of Medical Research (ICMR) charges no fee but rejects work already posted as a preprint.

## Preprint servers
| Server | Best for | Time to post | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| medRxiv (https://www.medrxiv.org/about/FAQ) | Clinical, gastro-hepatology, NHANES/MIMIC, LLM-in-medicine | 2–4 days | Needs ethics/IRB statement (public de-identified data: state exempt); no case reports or narrative reviews |
| arXiv (https://info.arxiv.org/help/endorsement.html) | Imaging AI (cs.CV, eess.IV), LLMs (cs.CL), security (cs.CR) | 1–4 days | First-time submitters need an endorsement |
| bioRxiv (https://www.biorxiv.org/about/FAQ) | Non-clinical computational pathology methods | ~3 days | Clinical work redirected to medRxiv |
| Research Square In Review | Springer/BMC submissions (opt-in) | Up to ~3 weeks | Posted only after the journal's check |

Elsevier allows preprints anywhere except some society-owned or double-anonymised titles (https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/sharing). Springer journals generally allow preprints (verify per journal).
